# Submission release review

The merged museums-v2 architecture, rival tuning, connection recovery, and getaway presentation are live. Only changes that meet the requested gates are eligible for further deployment.

## Independent assessment

- Delaying the Crown prevents the opening rush, but it also concentrates traffic at one late objective. The current timed-vault candidate passes the 40-seed early-pickup check, yet the guard posted from the start defeats the existing Echo plate handoff requirement (25/60 versus 45/60). It is not ready to ship.
- A siren located at the laser emitter is clearer spatially, but moving noise changes guard diversion. The candidate makes co-op Normal and Pro substantially easier; its mechanical unit tests passing is insufficient approval.
- A getaway is suitable presentation polish: no bank, clock, actor position, or outcome changes. Reduced-motion users go directly to the recap. Winning vans leave first; stranded actors remain visible.
- Shuffling art can break memorized routes only if the pedestal pools preserve meaningful risk and useful hiding routes. The first 14-pedestal deal fails those constraints and balance. Merely randomizing coordinates is not an adequate architectural redesign.
- Echo interception must be a deliberate exception for the carrier's pursuer. It must retain wall clearance, avoid unrelated patrols, preserve art-carrying priority, and reset its eight-second cooldown between matches. The controlled two-second-lead fixture measures actual banking, not just whether a radio line plays.
- Archive darkness changes gameplay, not just rendering. The renderer and guard prediction must use the same lamp definitions. In combination with the defender it misses Rookie difficulty and one real six-player return-to-van case, despite passing isolated balance checks.
- Rotating Crossroads lasers and top docks are explicitly dependent on 1–6 passing. They are not included while the preceding gates remain unmet.

## Connection limits

Cloudflare STUN, a twelve-second initial Direct attempt, one ICE restart, and late Direct promotion are implemented without accounts. The network checker reports server-reflexive candidates, comparable two-server mappings when the browser exposes them, and a real local DataChannel round trip. Equal mappings are a clue, not proof of reachability. The cloud-browser check produced no srflx candidate and timed out its local loopback. This does not establish whether a particular player's home network supports Direct; STUN does not replace TURN on restrictive networks.

## Evidence conventions

The difficulty policy is the same naive collector with Auto Echo, seeds 1001–1040, forty matches per cell; this is a regression benchmark, not a human-skill prediction. CPU uses 2250 ticks per layout, measured separately from concurrent simulation jobs. Museum screenshots are labeled frozen development fixtures, not live multiplayer playtests. Intentional rescue waits require both the wait-rescue task and an actually tied teammate; other idle cases remain failures. Mirrored fairness compares equal-value mirrored opportunities and complete collection routes, not unequal distances to the very same side exhibit.
