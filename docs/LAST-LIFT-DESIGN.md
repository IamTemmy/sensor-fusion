# Last Lift design contract

Entertainment goal: get players moving immediately, arguing about another haul, and remembering who betrayed whom. No quizzes or educational objectives.

Story: the Halden Museum of Impossible Things enters lockdown at midnight. Three portable exhibits are worth $5,000 in fictional scoring. Players need another thief to carry each, but negotiate the payout privately at the lift. Banking requires leaving the job permanently. Stolen loot is marked hot and must cross the map to a second exit.

The first slice is intentionally bounded: one floor, two patrols, three exhibits, two exits, two decoys per thief. No shooting, weapon upgrades, inventory trees or sprawling campaign. Public radio is preset text; actual friends can use their own call.

The UI must expose all consequences before commitment. Steal cannot retroactively take someone else's already banked money. Mutual betrayal loses that exhibit only. No hidden departure: an explicit OUT tag prevents remaining players searching for a disconnected or escaped partner. No player can remotely force a teammate to cash out.

The modeled alarm and patrol rules are fictional arcade mechanics, not real-world security instruction. SVG art and code are original. No third-party game art/code was copied.
