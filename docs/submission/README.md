**Description (ready to paste):**

Quiet hours. Loud company. Velmont: After Hours is a multiplayer museum heist playable in a browser. Share a room code, assemble your crew, and steal art together—or compete against a rival crew. Sound is part of the strategy: steal an artwork and its alarm follows you; throw its noise elsewhere or hand it to a teammate to create an escape window. Coordinate at the security plate to reach the Crown, navigate distinct museum layouts, and make it back to your van before time runs out.

Play with friends or fill crew slots with bots. Desktop and phone controls, practice, adjustable lighting and three difficulties make the heist easy to enter while leaving room for teamwork, risky rescues and better plans on the next run.

## Submit manually in Handshake (desktop)

1. Open the **Create a Multiplayer Game** mission using the Handshake account associated with your entry.
2. Complete/check the Build steps, then select **Submit Project** in the chapter list.
3. Enter the title, playable URL, description and cover image above.
4. Choose whether to show the project in the AI Showcase. The general help article recommends it; the contest rules do not list it as a separate required entry field.
5. Submit, then verify the confirmation and project tile. Save a screenshot of the confirmation for yourself.

**Deadline:** the linked Official Rules say October 30, 2026, 11:59 PM Pacific (October 31, 1:59 AM Central). The marketing mission page says October 31; use the earlier official deadline.

**Video:** the contest rules list title, cover image, description and project URL—not a required video. If the actual submission form presents another mandatory field, follow that form. An optional 30–60 second clip can show joining a room, teamwork with the alarm, a Crown escape and the result screen. Do not delay the required entry for a video.

## Before clicking Submit

- Open the public URL in a fresh window and confirm it loads without a game account.
- Have one friend join using the room code or invite link; start a short match.
- Keep the host tab open during multiplayer.
- Check the uploaded cover and full URL in the submission preview.
- Submit before the earlier official deadline and retain the confirmation.
